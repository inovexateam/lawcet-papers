Maths Formulas

All arithmetic shortcuts — memorise once, apply fast in exam.

Percentage & Profit/LossHigh Priority
Percentage change = (New−Old) / Old × 100
Profit % = Profit / CP × 100
SP (profit known) = CP × (100 + P%) / 100
SP (loss known) = CP × (100 − L%) / 100
CP from SP & profit% = SP × 100 / (100 + P%)
Trick: Two items sold at same SP, one at x% profit & one at x% loss → always a LOSS. Loss% = (x²/100)%
Simple & Compound Interest
SI = P × R × T / 100
Amount (SI) = P + SI = P(1 + RT/100)
CI Amount = P × (1 + R/100)ᵀ
CI − SI for 2 yrs = P × (R/100)²
CI − SI for 3 yrs = P(R/100)²(3 + R/100)
Quick check: 2 yrs at 10% → SI=20%, CI=21%. Difference is always SI on SI of first year.
Time, Speed & Distance
Speed = Distance / Time
Avg speed (equal dist) = 2xy / (x+y) (harmonic mean — NOT arithmetic)
Relative speed (same dir) = |s1 − s2|
Relative speed (opp dir) = s1 + s2
km/h → m/s: × 5/18 | m/s → km/h: × 18/5
Train crossing platform: Time = (Train + Platform length) / Speed
Boat & Stream: Downstream = u+v | Upstream = u−v | Speed of boat = (D+U)/2 | Speed of stream = (D−U)/2
Ratio, Proportion & Mixtures
If a:b = p:q → aq = bp
Alligation rule: (d−m) : (m−c) where d=dearer, c=cheaper, m=mean
Partnership profit ∝ Capital × Time
Compound ratio of a:b and c:d = ac : bd
Alligation cross: Write dearer top-left, cheaper top-right, mean in center. Subtract diagonally → ratio of quantities.
Number Series Patterns
AP: a, a+d, a+2d... | nth term = a + (n−1)d
GP: a, ar, ar²... | nth term = arⁿ⁻¹
Sum of AP = n/2 × (2a + (n−1)d)
Squares: 1,4,9,16,25,36,49,64,81,100,121,144,169,196,225
Cubes: 1,8,27,64,125,216,343,512,729,1000
Series check order: 1) Constant diff? (AP) → 2) Constant ratio? (GP) → 3) Diffs form a series? (2nd order) → 4) Squares/cubes?
Permutation, Combination & Probability
nPr = n! / (n−r)! (arrangements, ORDER matters)
nCr = n! / r!(n−r)! (selections, order irrelevant)
Probability = Favourable / Total outcomes
P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A∩B)
P(A and B) independent = P(A) × P(B)
Cards deck: 52 cards, 4 suits (13 each), 2 colours | Dice: 6 faces | nC0 = nCn = 1 | nC1 = n

Error Spotting — Common TrapsHigh Priority
Neither/Either → singular verb: "Neither of them is correct"
Less vs Fewer: Less = uncountable | Fewer = countable: "Fewer lawyers, less justice"
Between vs Among: Between = 2 | Among = 3+
Lay vs Lie: Lay = to place (transitive) | Lie = to recline (intransitive)
Each/Every/None/Anyone → always singular verb
Collective nouns (jury, committee, team) → singular when acting as one unit
One of the + plural noun + singular verb: "One of the judges was present"
Who/Whom: Who = subject | Whom = object: "To whom it may concern"
FANBOYS (coordinating conjunctions): For, And, Nor, But, Or, Yet, So

Mental Ability

Reasoning, series, coding, directions, syllogisms — all quick-solve tricks here.

Coding-DecodingHigh Priority
EJOTY trick: E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25 → instantly know any letter's position!
Opposite letter pair: A↔Z, B↔Y, C↔X... (pair sum always = 27)
Letter position: A=1, B=2... Z=26 | Reverse: A=26, B=25... Z=1
If +3 shift: A→D, B→E, C→F (Caesar cipher)
Check: is it number coding, letter coding, symbol coding or mixed? Identify pattern type first.
Blood Relations — StrategyHigh Priority
Golden rule: ALWAYS draw a family tree on paper. Never solve in your head.
Mother's brother = Maternal uncle | Father's sister = Paternal aunt
Assign gender immediately: "wife" → female, "husband" → male, "son" → male
Generation indicators: parent (1 up), grandparent (2 up), child (1 down), grandchild (2 down)
If A is B's mother's only brother's daughter → A is B's maternal uncle's daughter = cousin
Directions & Sitting ArrangementsMedium
Compass: "Never Eat Stale Wheat" → N, E, S, W (clockwise)
Sunrise = East shadow points West | Sunset = West shadow points East
If facing North: left = West, right = East
If facing South: left = East, right = West (reversed!)
Circular arrangement: face centre → left/right same as straight line. Back to centre → reversed.
Sitting tip: Draw a circle/row diagram for every arrangement question. Never visualise mentally.
SyllogismsMedium
Combination rules: All+All=All | All+No=No | Some+All=Some | Some+No=Some Not | No+All=No
"All A are B" → some B are A (conversion) but NOT all B are A
"Some A are B" → some B are A (always true)
"No A is B" → No B is A (always reversible)
Venn diagram method: draw circles for all terms, check if conclusion is possible/definite/impossible
In "either-or" conclusions: both must be individually false but their combination true. Check carefully.
Analogies & Odd One OutMedium
Find relationship: tool→use, part→whole, cause→effect, worker→product, word→synonym
For number analogies: try +, −, ×, ÷, squares, cubes, prime numbers
Odd one out: find what's common among 3, the different one is answer
Check multiple properties: shape, colour, function, category — odd one could be on any dimension
